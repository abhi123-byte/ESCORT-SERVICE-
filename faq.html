FAQ

Frequently asked questions about LUXE bookings.

What type of work can I book talent for?

Photography, advertising, editorial, brand campaigns, digital content and other legitimate professional projects.

How does booking work?

Choose a profile, submit your project details and contact information, then confirm availability and commercial terms directly with the platform or talent representative.

Can I add my own profiles?

Yes. Add profile objects in js/profiles.js and upload matching images to the images folder.

Are the sample profiles real?

No. The included names and profile information are sample content for demonstrating the website structure.
